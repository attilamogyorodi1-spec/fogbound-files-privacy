Privacy Policy – Fogbound Files

Effective date: 28 October 2026

Fogbound Files is a YouTube mystery channel run by Attila M. "Fogbound Files" is also the name of a small, personal, single-user tool that the channel owner uses to upload and schedule the channel's own videos and to read the channel's own YouTube Analytics. It is not a public service and has no other users.

Whose Google account is used

Only the channel owner's own Google account is used. No one else is asked to sign in or grant access.

What data is accessed

The tool accesses only the owner's own YouTube channel data: video uploads, video metadata (titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, privacy and scheduling settings) and the channel's analytics.

How data is used and shared

This data is used only to manage and monitor the owner's own channel. Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with, disclosed to, or transferred to third parties.

Storage

Access tokens are stored privately on the owner's own computer. They are not published or sent to anyone else.

Data from other users

No data from other users is collected.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
